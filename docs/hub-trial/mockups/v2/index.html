Ask on WhatsApp
Who can request a trial box?

Gift, souvenir and tourist shops in South Florida — ZIP codes 33001 to 33499. One box per business.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The box is free and yours to keep and sell. No payment, no contract.

What happens after the trial?

Reorder when you’re ready. Reorders are wholesale with a $150 minimum, paid on delivery or by card. Terms are available after your first paid order.

When will my box arrive?

We reply within 2 business days. Once approved, your box ships from Miami in [placeholder] days.

Do I need a resale certificate?

Not for the trial. Have your Florida resale certificate ready for your first paid order.

My shop is outside South Florida.

Send the form anyway. Trial boxes are South Florida only for now, but we’ll reply about wholesale.
